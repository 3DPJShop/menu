About 3DPJ

🦝 Hey, I'm Julius — part-time raccoon, full-time 3D printing nerd.

In February 2025 I broke my arm. While I was recovering, I won a 3D printer — and fell hard for designing and printing. What started as a hobby is now a small Toronto shop.

I design in CAD and Fusion 360 and print tap-to-share NFC keychains, custom pendants and photo frames. Glow-in-the-dark, quirky and clever stuff, made with care.
