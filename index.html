About 3DPJ

🦝 Hey, I'm Julius: part-time raccoon, full-time 3D printing nerd, and a Toronto small business.

In February 2025 I broke my arm. While I was recovering I won a 3D printer and fell in love with it. I started printing, then scaling up.

I actually went to school for mechatronics engineering, but the part I truly enjoyed was the modelling and CAD.

Today I design using Fusion 360 and make NFC keychains, pendants, custom framed photo packages and custom prints. Pretty much anything can be printed. If you have an STL file, or just an idea, I can make it happen.

My goal is to make big art pieces. And if you want anything 3D-printed, just let me know.
